Projects

Review Report – Emerging Trends in 5G and Beyond

Co-authored a technical review exploring the evolution from 5G to 6G, focusing on terahertz communication, intelligent antennas, beamforming, and energy-efficient architectures.

  • Reviewed key trends in terahertz communication and intelligent antenna systems.
  • Analysed design challenges in antenna miniaturization and propagation at higher frequencies.
  • Studied the integration of AI/ML for adaptive and high-throughput wireless networks.

Course: 5G Fundamentals and Architectures

BCH Encoder & Decoder Simulation

Implemented a BCH encoder and decoder (15,7) using NumPy and simulated transmission over a noisy channel.

  • Simulated bit flips over a noisy channel.
  • Evaluated bit error rate and basic error correction performance.
  • Used syndrome decoding for error correction.

Tools: Python, NumPy

Used Car Price Prediction

Developed a machine learning regression model to predict used car prices based on vehicle features.

  • Used features such as year, mileage, make, model, and engine power.
  • Performed exploratory data analysis and model evaluation.
  • Reported performance using R² score and mean absolute error.

Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn

Bluetooth Controlled Car

Developed a mobile-controlled vehicle using the Intel 8051 microcontroller and Bluetooth communication for wireless navigation and control.

Course: Microprocessor Lab

Audio Triggered Light Switch

Designed and implemented a sound-activated light control circuit on a breadboard using IC 4017 and op-amp 741.

Course: Communication Networks Lab
