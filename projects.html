Projects

Review Report – Emerging Trends in 5G and Beyond

Overview: Co-authored a technical review on the evolution from 5G to 6G, focusing on terahertz communication, intelligent antennas, beamforming, energy-efficient architectures, and the role of AI/ML in next generation wireless systems.

My contribution: Studied antenna and propagation challenges at higher frequencies, including miniaturization, beamforming, and high-frequency link reliability. Also reviewed how AI/ML can support adaptive and high-throughput wireless networks.

Course: 5G Fundamentals and Architectures

Topics: 5G, 6G, terahertz communication, intelligent antennas, beamforming, energy-efficient networks, AI/ML for wireless systems.

BCH Encoder & Decoder Simulation

Overview: Implemented a BCH encoder and decoder for a (15,7) code using Python and NumPy, and simulated transmission over a noisy channel.

My contribution: Built the encoding and decoding flow, simulated bit errors, implemented syndrome-based error correction, and evaluated basic bit error rate behaviour under different noise conditions.

Course: Error Control Coding

Tools / Concepts: Python, NumPy, BCH codes, syndrome decoding, noisy channel simulation, bit error rate analysis.
