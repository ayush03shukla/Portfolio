About

I am a Research Associate at IIIT Delhi working on foreign object debris detection using millimeter-wave FMCW radar. My current work focuses on low-rank and sparse signal processing methods, clutter suppression, range-angle processing, and OS-CFAR detection for runway safety applications.

Research Interests

  • mmWave radar sensing
  • Radar signal processing
  • FMCW radar and MIMO radar
  • Electromagnetics and EMC/EMI modeling
  • Antennas and RF sensing systems

Selected Projects

Low-Rank and Sparse FOD Detection with 77 GHz mmWave Radar

Developed an ADMM-based low-rank plus sparse decomposition pipeline for detecting low-RCS foreign object debris on runway-like surfaces using TI AWR1843BOOST FMCW radar data.

Surrogate Modeling for EMI Filter Insertion Loss

Compared Kriging, PCE, PCK, and wideband Kriging models for estimating EMI filter insertion loss, with work published at IEEE EDAPS 2023.

Wearable WBAN Antenna Design

Designed and analyzed a wearable antenna for wireless body-area network applications using full-wave EM simulation.
