About Me

Hi! I am Ayush, I am a Research Associate in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIIT Delhi, working on millimeter-wave radar-based foreign object debris detection for airport runway safety, in collaboration with the Indian Air Force.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in Electrical and Computer Engineering, with research interests in radar signal processing, mmWave sensing, applied electromagnetics, and sparse reconstruction.

My research interests are radar signal processing, mmWave radar sensing, target detection and estimation, signal processing, sparse reconstruction, computational electromagnetics, antennas, RF systems, surrogate modelling,

I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Manipal Institute of Technology in 2024. My bachelor’s thesis focused on the work carried out at IFE, TU Graz, on the designing surrogate models for electromagnetic compatibility analysis of power electronic circuits.
