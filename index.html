About Me

Hi! I am Ayush, I am a Research Associate in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIIT Delhi. Currently I am working on 77 GHz frequency-modulated continuous-wave radar-based foreign object debris detection for airport runway safety, in collaboration with the Indian Air Force, the air arm of the Indian Armed Forces.

My current work focuses on low-RCS target detection in ground clutter using radar signal processing, BPM-MIMO, range-angle processing, background subtraction, radar calibration, low-rank and sparse reconstruction, ADMM, and OS-CFAR detection.

I received my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Manipal Institute of Technology in 2024. My bachelor’s thesis focused on designing surrogate models for electromagnetic compatibility analysis of power electronic circuits.
