About Me

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at IIIT Delhi, working on millimeter-wave radar-based foreign object debris detection for runway safety applications.

My work focuses on FMCW radar signal processing, MIMO radar, range-angle processing, clutter suppression, low-rank and sparse reconstruction, and detection algorithms such as OS-CFAR. My broader background includes applied electromagnetics, EMC/EMI modeling, surrogate modeling, and antenna design.
