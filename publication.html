Conference Publications

[1] Low Rank and Sparsity Based Foreign Object Debris Detection with Millimeter Wave Radar

Authors: A. Shukla, S. S. Ram

Venue: IEEE Radar Conference 2026, Phoenix, Arizona, USA, 2026

This work proposes a low-rank and sparse decomposition framework for detecting low-RCS foreign object debris using millimeter-wave radar. The approach models structured ground clutter as a low-rank component and foreign object debris targets as sparse components in range-angle space.

Keywords: mmWave radar, FMCW radar, foreign object debris, low-rank and sparse decomposition, ADMM, clutter suppression, target detection.

[2] Comparison of Surrogate Modelling Approaches for Estimation of EMI Filter Insertion Loss

Authors: A. Shukla, S. S. Nukala, Akash, D. K. Singh, D. Gope, J. Hansen

Venue: IEEE EDAPS 2023, Mauritius, 2023

This work compares surrogate modelling approaches for estimating EMI filter insertion loss. The study evaluates Kriging, Polynomial Chaos Expansion, Polynomial Chaos Kriging, and Wideband Kriging methods for simulation-driven EMC/EMI modelling.

Keywords: EMI/EMC, insertion loss, surrogate modelling, Kriging, Polynomial Chaos Expansion, Wideband Kriging, uncertainty quantification.

Learning-Based Extensions for Low-Rank and Sparse Radar Sensing

Ongoing work extending the sparse optimization framework for FOD detection toward learning-based unfolded methods. The goal is to improve robustness while preserving interpretability from the underlying ADMM-based radar signal processing pipeline.
