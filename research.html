Research Areas

Radar Signal Processing and mmWave Sensing

My current research focuses on FMCW radar, MIMO radar, range-angle processing, target detection and estimation, radar calibration, detection map generation, and low-RCS target detection.

Sparse Reconstruction and Clutter Suppression

I work on low-rank and sparse decomposition, ADMM-based optimization, structured ground clutter separation, sparse target recovery, and OS-CFAR detection.

Computational Electromagnetics and EMC/EMI

My previous work involved surrogate modelling, Gaussian Processes, Kriging, uncertainty quantification, Feature Selective Validation, and EMC-aware modelling of power electronic circuits.

Antennas and RF Systems

I have also worked on UWB antenna design, wearable antennas, RF sensing systems, ANSYS HFSS-based electromagnetic simulation, and SAR-aware antenna design.

Learning-Based Radar Sensing

I am currently extending sparse optimization frameworks toward learning-based unfolded methods for more robust radar sensing.
