Research Areas

I am broadly interested in applied electromagnetics, especially where systems, sensing, and signal processing come together for real-world engineering problems.

Radar and mmWave Sensing

  • Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) radar
  • Low-RCS target detection in high clutter ridden environment
  • Radar calibration and measurement

Signal Processing for Detection

I am interested in how signal processing can make weak targets visible in noisy or cluttered measurements. In my current work, I have explored background subtraction, clutter suppression, sparse reconstruction, and detection methods for radar data.

  • Clutter suppression
  • Sparse reconstruction
  • Very low Signal-to-noise (SNR) ratio detection

EMC/EMI and Surrogate Modelling

  • EMI filter insertion loss estimation
  • Gaussian Process and Kriging models
  • Uncertainty-aware simulation workflows
  • Feature Selective Validation

Antennas and RF Systems

  • Ultra-Wideband antenna (UWB) design
  • Wearable antennas
  • Full-wave EM simulation
  • Radiation and bending analysis

Learning-Based Extensions

More recently, I have started exploring how learning-based methods can be connected with model-based radar algorithms.

  • Model-based learning
  • Unfolded optimization methods
  • Learning-enhanced sparse sensing
